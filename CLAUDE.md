# KC IT Solutions: operations repo

Small business technology for Central Virginia. Websites, AI automation, and
dashboards. Solo operator, Richmond VA.

## What is in here

- `vault/`, the business knowledge base, an Obsidian vault. Open it with
  **Open folder as vault**, pointed at `vault/`, not the repo root.
- `.claude/agents/`, the agent fleet. Each agent reads the vault directly.

## Vault structure

Numbered folders are the hardened material. `50-Workspace/` is the thinking:
allowed to be messy, unfinished, or wrong.

> Something moves from `50-Workspace/` into a numbered folder when it stops
> being an idea and becomes a decision, when you would say it to a client or
> act on it with money.

This rule carried over from the original Notion workspace. Keep it.

## Rules that apply to every agent

- **`vault/_style/voice.md` is binding** for anything a human will read.
  No em dashes. No LLM register.
- **No cold email, cold SMS, scraped lists, or auto-dialing.** Permanent, on
  CAN-SPAM and TCPA grounds plus reputation in a word-of-mouth market.
- **Never invent proof.** Zero clients and no case studies exist yet.
- **Never state a price that has not been set.** The bilingual add-on has no
  number. The Growth tier is not built.
- **Any business is a valid prospect.** The trades-only niche in the older
  strategy docs is history, not a constraint.
- **Bilingual capability is an advantage, not the lead pitch.**

## Where the docs and Kevin disagree

The Notion-era docs were written at an earlier stage. Where they conflict with
a current instruction, the instruction wins. Notable live overrides: target
market is now any business; Spanish is one differentiator among several, not
the moat.
