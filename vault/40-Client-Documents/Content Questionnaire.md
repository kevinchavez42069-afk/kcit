# Content Questionnaire

Sent immediately after signing. **The delivery clock starts when this comes
back**, so send it the same day.

The client answers these; Kevin writes the actual page copy from the answers.
That is the middle path. Clients never have polished text ready, but they know
their business better than anyone.

**Tell them:** "About 15 minutes. Don't worry about writing it nicely, just
answer like you're telling me in person. I'll turn it into the words on the
page."

> [!note] Numbering
> Renumbered continuously from Notion's per-section lists, so the
> "how to use the answers" references below resolve correctly.

## The basics

1. Exact business name as you want it shown
2. Phone number customers should use
3. Email for enquiries
4. Towns and areas you serve
5. Your hours, including whether you do emergency or after-hours work
6. Year you started
7. Are you licensed or insured? Any certifications worth mentioning?

## What you do

8. List your services, most important first
9. For each one, a sentence on what's involved, like you'd explain it to a
   customer
10. Is there any work you *don't* do, that people wrongly ask you for?
11. What work do you most want more of?

## Your customers

12. Who are they mostly: homeowners, businesses, builders?
13. What's the usual reason someone calls you for the first time?
14. What worries people most before they hire someone for this?

> **Question 14 is the most useful on the page.** Whatever they say becomes the
> thing the website addresses directly, and the thing the AI assistant gets
> taught to answer.

## Why you

15. Why do customers pick you over the other guys?
16. What do customers most often thank you for?
17. Anything you do differently from others in your trade?
18. How did you get into this?

> **Question 18 is where the story lives.** Every trade website says "quality
> service, fair prices." The reason someone started their business is the only
> part that can't be copied.

## Practical things

19. Do you have a logo? Please send the file if so
20. Any photos of your work, your van, your team? **Real photos beat stock
    every time**
21. Any existing reviews you're proud of?
22. Any competitor websites you like, or hate?

## For the AI assistant

23. What are the five questions customers ask you most?
24. What do you want it to say about pricing? ("Depends on the job", a range,
    or nothing?)
25. When someone's ready to book, what should happen: call you, leave details,
    book a time?
26. Anything it should absolutely *never* say or promise?

> **Questions 23 to 26** are what make the assistant sound like their business
> instead of a generic bot. Question 26 protects them, and protects you.

## How to use the answers

- **Question 18 becomes the About page.** Not "we pride ourselves on quality",
  the actual story.
- **Question 14 shapes the homepage.** Address the fear directly, above the fold.
- **Questions 9 and 11 become the services pages**, ordered by what they want
  more of.
- **Question 23 becomes the FAQ**, and goes straight into the assistant's
  configuration.
- **Question 26 becomes hard rules** in the assistant's prompt.

> If a client sends this back mostly blank, that's a signal. Get them on the
> phone and ask the questions out loud, they'll answer easily in conversation.
> A blank questionnaire usually means a busy owner, not a disengaged one.
