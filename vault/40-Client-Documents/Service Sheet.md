## Why it's written this way

- **The problem comes before the price.** They have to recognise themselves in
  the first three lines or the rest doesn't get read.
- **No jargon.** Not "responsive design" but "works on phones". Not "SEO" but
  "shows up in local search". Not "chatbot" but "answers customers day and
  night".
- **Prices are visible.** Most competitors hide them behind "contact us", and
  owners hate that. Showing them is a differentiator.
- **The QR is the real close.** It turns a piece of paper into a live demo.
  Nothing else on the sheet works as hard.
- **The monthly plan is on the sheet from the start**, so it is never a
  surprise later.
