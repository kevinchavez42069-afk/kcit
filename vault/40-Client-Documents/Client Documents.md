# Client Documents

The documents used to sell and deliver work, in the order they get used.

| # | Document | When it's used |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [[Service Sheet]] | Handed over in person during a walk-in |
| 2 | [[Discovery Questions]] | The conversation that qualifies them, before quoting |
| 3 | [[Proposal Template]] | Sent a day or two after a good conversation |
| 4 | [[Founding Client Agreement]] | Signed before work starts, first two clients only |
| 5 | [[Content Questionnaire]] | Sent immediately after signing. Starts the delivery clock |

> [!warning] Not legal advice
> These are plain-language drafts, and plain-language drafts are far better
> than nothing. Before signing a real client, have a Virginia attorney review
> the agreement, or at minimum use a vetted template service. Contracts are
> cheap to review and expensive to get wrong.
