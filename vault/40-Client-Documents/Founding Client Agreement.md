# Founding Client Agreement

> [!warning] Not legal advice
> A plain-language draft. Have a Virginia attorney review it before signing a
> real client, or use a vetted template service. Contracts are cheap to review
> and expensive to get wrong.
>
> **Also: form the LLC first.** Signing agreements as an unregistered sole
> proprietor means no separation between business and personal liability.

For the **first two discounted clients only.** After that, standard terms at
full price. Replace everything in `[brackets]`.

## The agreement

> # Founding Client Agreement
>
> **Between:** KC IT Solutions ([Kevin Chavez / KC IT Solutions LLC]),
> Richmond, Virginia
> **And:** [Business Name], [Address]
> **Date:** [Date]
>
> ## 1. What I'm building
>
> **[Package] website**, [X] pages, built to work on phones and computers.
>
> Included:
> - [List exactly what's in it]
> - [AI assistant, if applicable]
> - [Google Business Profile setup, if applicable]
>
> **Not included** unless agreed separately in writing: additional pages,
> e-commerce or online payments, logo design, professional photography,
> ongoing content writing.
>
> ## 2. Founding client pricing
>
> You are one of **two founding clients**. In exchange for the reduced price
> below, you agree to section 5.
>
> | | |
> |---|---|
> | Normal price | $[800/1,200/1,800] |
> | **Your price** | **$[500]** |
> | Deposit due to start | $[100-200] |
> | Balance due on completion | $[remainder] |
>
> **Monthly plan:** $99/month instead of the standard $149, for as long as you
> stay on it. First [1/3] month[s] included.
>
> This pricing is limited to founding clients and is not offered afterwards.
>
> ## 3. Timeline
>
> Your site live within **1-2 weeks** of receiving your completed content
> questionnaire. The clock starts when the questionnaire comes back, not at
> signing. I can't write your pages before I know about your business.
>
> ## 4. Changes
>
> **Two rounds of revisions are included.** A round means you send me your
> changes together and I make them.
>
> Further changes are billed at $[75]/hour, agreed with you in advance. You'll
> never get a surprise invoice.
>
> ## 5. What I'm asking for in return
>
> This is what the founding discount is for. You agree to:
>
> 1. **Provide a short written testimonial** within two weeks of the site going
>    live, describing your honest experience.
> 2. **Allow me to show your website** as an example of my work.
> 3. **Allow me to name [Business Name]** as a client.
> 4. **Take a reference call** from a prospective client, if asked, no more
>    than [three] times a year.
>
> If you're genuinely unhappy with the work, you're not obliged to give a
> positive testimonial. An honest one, or none, is fine. I'd rather fix the
> problem.
>
> ## 6. What you own
>
> **You own your domain name**, registered in your name. If you ever leave, it
> goes with you.
>
> **You own the content and images** you provide, and the finished website
> design once paid in full.
>
> I keep hosting and technical setup on my account while you're on the monthly
> plan. If you leave, I'll hand over the site files so another developer can
> take it on.
>
> ## 7. The monthly plan
>
> **$99/month** covers hosting, the AI assistant running, and small changes.
>
> **Small changes means:** updating text and images, hours, prices, or adding a
> service to an existing page.
>
> **It does not mean:** new pages, redesigns, or new features. Those are quoted
> separately.
>
> Either of us can cancel with 30 days' notice. No long contract.
>
> ## 8. Payment
>
> Deposit to start. Balance on completion, before the site goes live. Monthly
> billing begins when the included period ends.
>
> ## 9. If it isn't working out
>
> Either of us can walk away in writing. If you cancel after work has started,
> the deposit covers work done to that point, and I'll invoice for any
> additional hours already spent. I'll always tell you before doing work that
> would be billed.
>
> **Agreed:**
>
> [Owner Name], [Business Name], Date
>
> Kevin Chavez, KC IT Solutions, Date

## Why it's written this way

- **Section 5 is the entire point.** The discount buys proof. Getting it in
  writing *before* work starts is the whole discipline. Asking afterwards is
  how people end up with nothing.
- **Section 5's escape clause matters.** Not demanding a *positive* testimonial
  keeps it honest and keeps you out of a fight if a project goes badly.
- **Section 3 protects the timeline** without lengthening the quote.
- **Section 6 removes the lock-in fight** before it can start.
- **Section 9 protects against the client who disappears** halfway through.
