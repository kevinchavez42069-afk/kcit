## Current web presence

No business-owned website found. Every source that comes up, Yellow Pages,
Yelp, Tripadvisor, Restaurantji, Sirved, Menupix, Zmenu, Wheree, is a
third-party directory page. One directory even lists a Yahoo local business
page as the "website" field, which is itself a sign there's no real site
behind it. No online ordering, no menu with prices anywhere but scanned
third-party listings.

## Lead-capture gap

The clearest sign here isn't a missed call, it's that the internet doesn't
agree on when this place is open. Directory listings conflict: some show
Monday-Friday 10am-4pm and closed weekends, others show Tuesday-Friday
10am-4pm with separate, shorter weekend hours (10:30am-3:30pm). Tripadvisor's
own listing shows "no hours available" at all. A locally-owned lunch spot
with genuinely positive reviews (5.0 on Tripadvisor, strongly rated on Yelp)
is invisible or wrong wherever a hungry person actually looks it up. Reviewer
commentary elsewhere specifically calls out the limited hours as
inconvenient.

## What one lost customer is worth

This is a lunch counter, not a big-ticket business: average ticket is likely
$10-15. The value isn't in one visit, it's in the regular. A weekly lunch
customer is worth $500-750 a year, and reviews describing a "history of
catering to the stars" and staff who "remember customers' names and orders"
point to a business built on exactly that kind of loyal regular, not
one-off traffic. The honest read is that the value case here is modest per
customer and rests on volume and repeat habit, not urgency.

**Estimate:** $500-1,000/year per regular customer, accumulating over years
of habitual lunch visits, not a single large job.

## Recommended package

**Standard.** The actual problem is that basic facts, hours above all, are
wrong or missing everywhere a customer would check, and there's no owned page
to point people to as the source of truth. A straightforward site with
accurate hours, the menu, and a Google Business Profile that's actually
claimed and correct fixes the real issue found here. At a $10-15 average
ticket, the AI chatbot and full Premium bundle isn't the honest sell, this
is a case for getting the basics right and stopping the directory confusion,
not for the highest-priced package.

## Opening line

"I looked you up before coming by and three different sites gave me three
different sets of hours for you, and one didn't have any hours listed at
all. If I can't tell when you're open, neither can anyone else searching for
lunch. Got a couple minutes?"
