## Current web presence

No dedicated website found across Facebook, Nextdoor, Yahoo Local, Yelp,
Wheree, and Loc8NearMe listings, every result is a third-party directory
page, not a business-owned site. Established 1987 by the current owner's
father, same location for roughly 30 years. Reputation is entirely word of
mouth and directory listings, nothing they control themselves.

## Lead-capture gap

Hours are Monday-Friday 6:15am-2:30pm, closed Saturday and Sunday, per
multiple directory listings. That's roughly 41 hours of availability out of
168 in a week, about a quarter of the time, and none of it falls on a
weekend, which is when most people actually think about getting their car
detailed. There's no way to see pricing, ask a question, or request an
appointment outside that window; no website, no online form, nothing but a
phone number that only picks up during those 41 hours.

## What one lost customer is worth

A full interior/exterior detail runs roughly $150-250, and repeat customers
typically come back every 2-4 months, call it 3-4 visits a year, so
$600-1,000/year from a retained customer. A shop that's held the same
customers for 30 years at the same address is clearly built on repeat
business and referrals, not one-off walk-ins, so a real client relationship
here is worth well over a thousand dollars across a few years.

**Estimate:** $2,000-3,000+ lifetime value per retained customer, built on
repeat visits rather than a single big-ticket job.

## Recommended package

**Standard.** The core problem here is invisibility and access, not
complexity: no site means no pricing, no hours anyone can trust outside a
narrow window, and no way to be found by someone searching after 2:30pm or on
a weekend. A basic site with services, pricing, hours, a contact form, and a
properly claimed Google Business Profile solves most of that on its own.
Premium's chatbot is a reasonable upsell later if he wants after-hours
scheduling, but a 30-year word-of-mouth shop doesn't clearly need the full
AI layer to fix the specific gap found here, and the honest starting
recommendation is the cheaper one.

## Opening line

"I noticed you're only open 6:15 to 2:30, Monday through Friday, and there's
no website anywhere, just directory listings. That means anyone who thinks
about getting their car detailed on a weekend or after work has no way to
reach you at all. I build sites for shops like yours so that doesn't cost you
business. Got a minute?"
