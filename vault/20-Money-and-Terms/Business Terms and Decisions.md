# Business Terms and Decisions

Decisions actually made, so they stop being re-litigated. Anything not on this
page is still open.

## Legal structure

**Not formalized.** No LLC, no registration, sole proprietorship by default.

> Worth addressing before signing real clients. A Virginia LLC costs roughly
> $100 to file plus a $50 annual fee. Not legal advice, but the cheapest risk
> reduction available before taking client money.

## Payment terms

| Client type | Deposit | Balance |
|---|---|---|
| Founding clients (first 2) | $100-200 token deposit | Remainder on completion |
| Standard clients | 50% | 50% on completion |

The founding-client deposit is deliberately small. It filters for people who
are serious while keeping the barrier low. A zero-barrier freebie attracts
people who will not show up for calls.

## Pricing

**$149/month is the standard listed price.** The value story is recovered
customers, not competing with cheap templates.

**Founding clients get ~$99/month**, framed as limited and temporary.

## Domain and hosting

**The client owns their domain. Kevin manages hosting.** Removes any lock-in
dispute before it can happen, and it reads as confident.

## Revisions

**Two rounds included on every build, then hourly.** Stated out loud on the
first call. It makes clients consolidate feedback instead of drip-feeding
changes for six weeks.

## What counts as an included "small change"

**Included:** text and image swaps, updating hours, updating prices, adding a
service to an existing page.

**Not included:** new pages, redesigns, new features, new functionality.

This line is what stops the $149 plan quietly becoming unlimited free work.

## Website copy

**Kevin writes the copy, from a structured content questionnaire the client
fills in.**

Not "the client supplies polished text," they never do and the project stalls.
Not "Kevin invents it from nothing," he doesn't know their business. The client
answers specific questions; Kevin turns those answers into the words.

## Turnaround

**1-2 weeks.** The clock starts **when the completed content questionnaire
comes back**, not at signing. The most common cause of a blown deadline is
waiting on the client.

## Business cards and QR code

The QR points at the live chat widget, so a prospect scans the card and the
product demonstrates itself. **This gates the entire week-one outreach plan.**
