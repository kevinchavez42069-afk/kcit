# Pricing and Unit Economics

## Current pricing

| Package | Price | Includes | Delivery |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $800 | 3 pages, mobile responsive, contact form, Google Business setup | 1 week |
| Standard | $1,200 | 5 pages, SEO basics, contact form, Google Business setup, 1 month hosting | 2 weeks |
| Premium | $1,800 | 5-7 pages, everything in Standard, AI chatbot, social automation, 3 months hosting, monthly check-in | 2 weeks |

## Is $1,800 too much?

No. If anything it is underpriced.

A small agency charges $3,000-10,000 for a 5-7 page site, and typically bills
$200-500/month separately for a chatbot. Premium bundles both for $1,800.

The discomfort comes from pricing *hours* rather than *outcome*. A business
with an $8,000 average job that misses five calls a week is buying recovered
jobs. One saved lead pays for Premium four times over.

**Going lower is a trap.** It attracts price shoppers, who are the most
demanding and slowest-paying clients, and a low price signals low quality.
Raising prices on existing clients later is miserable.

If $1,800 genuinely blocks a deal, split it: $900 to start, $900 at launch.
Same price, easier yes.

## The ceiling problem

**One-time only:** clearing $60k needs roughly 33 builds a year. At two weeks
each that is 66 weeks of work. It does not fit in a year.

**With recurring:** 30 clients at $149/month is about $54k/year, before
building anything new.

## The monthly plan

**$149/month**, beginning when the included hosting runs out. Includes hosting
and domain, the AI chatbot running, small site changes, and a monthly check-in.

### Cost to deliver, per client

| Item | Monthly cost |
|---|---|
| S3 + CloudFront hosting | ~$1-2 |
| Claude API (chatbot) | ~$2-8 |
| Time | 15-30 min for the check-in |

Roughly **$140 gross per client per month.** Thirty clients is about
$4,500/month recurring.

### Why the chatbot makes it sellable

"Maintenance" is a terrible product. Nobody feels it and they resent paying for
it. The chatbot is visibly on, costs real money to run, and produces leads the
client can count. That is a subscription an owner understands.

## The unpriced cliff

Standard includes 1 month hosting and Premium 3 months. **Month four currently
has no answer.** That cliff should become the on-ramp: the free months end, the
plan begins.
