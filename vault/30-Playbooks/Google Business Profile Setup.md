# Google Business Profile Setup

Everything prepared so the actual setup is ten minutes of typing. **Kevin
creates and verifies it himself.** Google requires the business owner to
verify, by design.

## Why this outweighs everything on the website

For local search, the Business Profile is the single largest ranking factor,
and it is free. Right now Google has almost nothing tying this business to
Central Virginia.

**Start it today even if you cannot finish it.** Postcard verification can take
days to arrive, so the clock matters more than the task.

## Before you begin

**Use a Google account you intend to keep.** Whoever owns that account controls
the listing.

## 1. The category, the most important choice on the page

The primary category is the strongest signal Google uses to decide which
searches you appear in, and the thing people most often get wrong.

### Recommended primary: Website Designer

Standard guidance for a mixed agency is to lead with Marketing Agency or
Internet Marketing Service *unless web design generates most of your revenue
and leads*, which here it does. Websites are the front-door product; the AI
assistant is attached to them.

It also matches what an owner actually types. Nobody searches "internet
marketing service near me." They search **"website designer richmond va."**

Revisit if monthly automation revenue ever outgrows build revenue.

### Recommended secondary categories, 2-3 only

1. Internet Marketing Service
2. Marketing Agency
3. Computer Support and Services, optional

> One specific primary plus two or three highly relevant secondaries. Do not
> add more. Google warns against category stuffing, and it dilutes rather than
> broadens.

## 2. Service-area business, get this right or your home address gets published

You work from home and travel to clients, so this is a **service-area
business**, not a storefront.

**When asked whether customers visit your location, answer no.** Select the
option for travelling to customers. Google requires you to hide the address if
you do not have a storefront with signage.

You clear the address fields and add service areas instead. Google keeps your
address on the back end for verification and still needs a mailing address for
the postcard, which stays private. The public profile shows name, phone,
website and service areas, but no street address.

> If you leave the service area blank, Google picks one for you. Do not let it.

### Service areas to enter

Richmond VA, Henrico County VA, Chesterfield County VA, Glen Allen VA,
Midlothian VA

> KC IT Solutions builds websites and AI assistants for small businesses across

> you're on a job, or someone messages at 9pm, and by the time you see it
> they've called somebody else. I build the website that brings people in and
> the assistant that answers them when you can't, so those jobs don't slip away.
>
> Based in Richmond and serving Henrico, Chesterfield, Glen Allen, Midlothian
> and the surrounding area. Websites start at $800, delivered in one to two
> weeks, with pricing published up front rather than hidden behind a quote form.
>
> Run by Kevin Chavez. You deal with me directly, not an account manager.

**Why it is written this way:** the problem comes before the service, prices are
stated, and it ends on the solo-operator angle rather than pretending to be an
agency.

objections and fills a section that otherwise sits empty.

## 7. The actual steps

| # | Step | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Go to google.com/business, sign in with the account you'll keep | 1 min |
| 2 | Enter business name **KC IT Solutions** | 1 min |
| 3 | Choose **Website Designer** as primary category | 1 min |
| 4 | When asked if customers visit you: **no, I travel to customers** | 1 min |
| 5 | Enter your address for verification, then add the service areas above | 2 min |
| 6 | Add phone (540) 327-2698 and website kcitsolutions.co | 1 min |
| 7 | Choose verification method and start it | 2 min |
| 8 | While waiting: paste the description, add services, secondary categories, upload the logo | 5 min |
| 9 | Once verified: post the Q&A above | 5 min |

Verification is usually postcard (up to 14 days), sometimes phone or video.
Take whatever is fastest.

> The profile stays unpublished until verification completes. That is normal.
> Fill everything in while you wait rather than waiting to start.

## 8. After it's live

- **Reviews are the single strongest ongoing factor.** Ask every founding client
- Post occasionally, monthly is enough
- Keep name, phone and address identical everywhere they appear. Consistency is
  a ranking signal
