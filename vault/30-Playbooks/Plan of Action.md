# Plan of Action

The sequenced version of what to do next. Strategy and kill criteria live in
[[The Plan]]; this is the order of operations.

Nobody can do these but Kevin. None of them are building. Total: under two hours.

- [ ] **Google Search Console.** Add the domain, verify, submit `sitemap.xml`.
      ~10 minutes. Every piece of SEO on the site is inert until this happens

      Verification arrives by mail and takes days
- [ ] **Form the Virginia LLC.** ~$100, online

      not just the homepage. The demo is the reason someone calls
- [ ] **Fix the Facebook cover.** `>Data%Reporting` should almost certainly be
      `>Data_Reporting`

> Google Business Profile is the highest-value item here. Someone searching
> "web designer Richmond VA" is ready to buy. Someone scrolling Facebook is not.

The first real objection is *"can I see something you've built?"* Right now the
honest answer is "my own website," and that loses the deal.

| Task | Why |

| **2-3 demo sites** | Clearly labelled as demos, not fake clients. Turns "I build websites" into "here, look" |

## Phase 2: Twenty conversations

**Not selling. Validating.**

1. Write down twenty local businesses
2. Walk in, or call. Not cold email, not cold SMS
3. Ask what happens to a call or message that comes in while they're busy.
   Listen, don't pitch
4. If they lean in, show them the demo on your phone. That is the whole pitch
5. Write down what they actually said, verbatim

**The number to track is conversations**, not followers, not posts, not pages
shipped.

> The demo is your opener, not your closer. "Can I show you something weird for
> thirty seconds?" gets further than any description of what you do.

## Phase 3: Founding clients

- Take **three founding clients** at a reduced rate, one possibly free, the
  others around $500
- The price you are paying is not the discount, it is the case study. Get
  permission in writing to use their name, results, and a photo
- Put every one on the monthly plan from day one

## Phase 4: Month 3 onward

- Charge full price: $800 / $1,200 / $1,800
- Sell the monthly plan on **every** deal
- Identify which single acquisition channel actually produced clients, drop the
  others

## Things that will feel productive and are not

| Trap | Why it's a trap |
|---|---|
| Chasing followers | Forty local owners who know your name beat 3,000 followers |
| Following pages for an "algorithm boost" | Following affects your feed, not your distribution |
| More website polish | The site is past the point where it is what's stopping a sale |
| Building the Growth tier | Nobody is on Essentials yet |
| Cold email or cold SMS | CAN-SPAM and TCPA. Ruled out permanently |

## Weekly rhythm

One question at the end of each week, written down:

> **How many business owners did I talk to this week?**
