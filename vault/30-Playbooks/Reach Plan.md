# Reach Plan

How to reach many people at once **without cold-contacting strangers**. Sits
alongside the ranking in [[Go-to-Market Strategy]], does not replace it.
In-person visits remain the number one channel.

## Why not mass cold email

Considered and rejected:

1. **Building the list by scraping is illegal in itself**, separately from
   whether the email complies. CAN-SPAM permits B2B commercial email; it does
   not permit how most people obtain the addresses.
2. **Reply rates to strangers run under 1-2%.** The volume needed to make that
   work causes the other three problems.
3. **Spam complaints risk blocklisting the sending domain**, which would also
   damage delivery of real lead emails and client correspondence.
4. **Reputation.** Central Virginia small business is a word-of-mouth market.

## 1. Local community groups

Cost $0. Join three to five relevant groups: Richmond-area small business
groups on Facebook, Henrico and Chesterfield community groups, Nextdoor.

Post genuine, non-salesy content. The missed-call problem, a practical tip, a
real question. Not "buy my service." Roughly every two weeks.

> One post reaches dozens of people who are in that group **by choice**.
> Nobody's inbox was invaded.

## 2. Small hand-picked outreach batch

Cost $0. Ten to fifteen businesses Kevin has an actual reason to contact: a
mutual connection, saw their work, someone mentioned them. Messaged
**individually, from his own inbox**, not a bulk tool.

## 3. SEO content pages

Cost $0, one-time project. Two or three pages answering what owners search:

- How much should a website cost in Richmond, VA
- Do I need a website, or is a Facebook page enough
- What an AI chat assistant on a small business site actually does

## 4. Chamber of commerce

~$200-500/year. Defer until there is revenue. The payoff is **list access**: a
newsletter mention or member-directory listing reaches an entire membership
through a channel they already opted into.

## Sequencing

| Phase | When | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Now | Join 2-3 local groups. Draft one non-salesy test post. Start the shortlist |
| 2 | Next 2-4 weeks | Publish the first SEO page. Send the hand-picked batch individually |
| 3 | Once there is revenue | Evaluate a chamber or association |

**The principle:** reach people who already chose to be somewhere, or who you
have a real reason to contact. Everything past that turns into spam, and spam
costs more than it earns in a market this small.
