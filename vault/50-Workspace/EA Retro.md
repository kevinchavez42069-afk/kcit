# EA Retro

Weekly self-review, written by `executive-assistant`, appended by
`dashboard/scheduler.mjs` (`RETRO_DAY`/`RETRO_HOUR` in `dashboard/.env`,
default Sunday 7am). Chronological, new section at the bottom, same
convention as [[Activity Log]] and [[Push Log]].

This is the improvement loop from [[AI Operating System]] phase 6: EA
reviews the past week's standups and its own cost/usage data, then
proposes concrete changes — to its own prompt or another agent's — for
Kevin to review and apply himself. Nothing here gets applied
automatically. If a week produced nothing worth changing, the entry says
so plainly rather than inventing a suggestion to look productive.
