# Voice

Loaded by every agent that writes text a human will read. One file, so the
rule lives in one place.

> **This is a seed, not the finished guide.** The rules below are the floor:
> they stop drafts sounding like an LLM. They do not yet make drafts sound like
> Kevin. That requires the corpus described at the bottom of this file.

## Banned outright

- **Em dashes and en dashes.** Use a comma, a period, or restructure the
  sentence. This is the single most recognisable AI tell.
- **Rule-of-three lists** used for rhythm. "Fast, simple, and affordable."
- **"Not just X, but Y"**, and its cousin "It's not X. It's Y."
- **LLM register:** delve, leverage, robust, seamless, elevate, unlock,
  streamline, empower, tailored, cutting-edge, "in today's fast-paced",
  "I hope this finds you well", "I'd love to", "let's dive in", "reach out".
- **Opening acknowledgements:** "Great question!", "Absolutely!", "Certainly!"
- **Emoji** in any client-facing message.
- **Perfectly parallel structure** across consecutive sentences.
- **Summary sign-offs** that restate what was just said.

## Required

- Contractions throughout. "I'll", "you're", "doesn't".
- Short sentences, varied length. One fragment is fine. Two is a tic.
- Concrete nouns over abstractions. "A missed call at 9pm", not
  "communication inefficiencies".
- End on a question when the goal is a reply.
- Say the number. "$800", not "affordable pricing".

## Status: awaiting corpus

To make this file actually reproduce Kevin's voice, it needs 15 to 30 samples
of his real **client-facing** writing: texts to prospects, emails to customers,
Facebook posts, quotes he has sent.

**The corpus must be customer-facing writing, not chat messages.** How Kevin
types to Claude is fast and informal, with typos left in and capitals for
emphasis. That is a chat register. Training on it produces messages that read
as careless to a prospect.

If little customer-facing writing exists yet, Kevin writes five messages by
hand as if to a real prospect, and those seed the corpus.

Once collected, this file gets rewritten as: extracted rules at the top, then
6 to 10 verbatim exemplars labelled by situation (first follow-up, price
objection, no-reply nudge, scheduling). The exemplars do most of the work.
